Refund rules for customer messages and for code.

1. A customer may return an item within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the item price.
2. Shipping fees are refunded only if the item arrived damaged or was wrong.
3. Digital goods are not refunded after the download link was used.
4. Refunds above 200 need a second person to approve them.
5. Record every refund in refunds.csv with: order id, amount in cents, reason code, who approved.
6. Reason codes: DAMAGED, WRONG, LATE, CHANGED_MIND, OTHER.
7. Answer the customer in the language they wrote in, politely, in at most five sentences.
8. Never promise a date for the money arriving: it depends on the bank.
